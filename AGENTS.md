# Upside Lab agent notes

- Product name is **Upside Lab** (UI, metadata, docs), served at `upsidelab.app`. Legacy `portfell_*` table names and `portfell-*` localStorage keys stay as-is so the shared book / Save lock don’t break.
- **Do not invent or “fix” Aasad holdings** unless Martin pastes a new sheet. Canonical Aasad: cash −7000; NBIS 500@109.96; CRWV 1100@83.27; RKLB 200@68.65; BMNR 1500@18.20; VST 200@145. No NVDA/AVGO on Aasad. (This is only a guardrail against hallucinating Martin's own real data — it is not a product-wide bias; see next point.)
- **Generic product direction (2026-08-12):** the app is moving toward being usable by people outside the family (friends, eventually strangers). Per-ticker "house" Call %/Stock-Target baselines and the forecast engine's hardcoded per-ticker price-target floors (old `CALL_PCT_BASELINES` / `STOCK_TARGET_BASELINES` / `TICKER_BASE_MULTS`) have been **removed on purpose** — Call % now comes purely from each ticker's own realized volatility (`src/lib/market/write-plan.ts`), and forecast paths are reasoned per-ticker by the model with only a modest, generic sector-shaped fallback (`src/lib/forecast-conviction.ts`) for gaps. Do **not** reintroduce ticker-specific hardcoded bias/targets — Martin's own investing opinions belong in his own conviction notes/thesis fields, not in code every user shares.
- Not financial advice: Margus/Thesis Pulse/Forecast are framed as educational/informational scenario tools, not personalized investment advice. Keep disclaimer language intact when touching those surfaces (the wording lives in `src/lib/disclaimer.ts`; personas and prompts are under `src/lib/ai/`).

Never say sleeve, marks, tape, conviction, digestion, dry powder, beta, risk-on, drawdown, rotation. Say the plain thing (group of similar stocks, today's prices, why you own it). **Thesis is allowed** (Pulse badges: Thesis intact / Thesis watch / Thesis broken). - **Experience tier vs. options experience are two separate flags — do not blend them.** `experience_tier` (novice/investor/advanced, from onboarding Q1 blended with Q2) controls which *meta-tabs* are default-hidden (Lab, which contains Seasonality as a sub-tab; Pulse is top-level and shown to every tier on purpose), which Lab sub-tabs are hidden (`TIER_HIDDEN_LAB_TABS` — both tables are **empty for every tier** as of 2026-08-18 per a product call, so nothing is tier-hidden today; the machinery is live and one entry brings it back), and panel default-visibility. `knows_options` (`src/lib/experience-tier.ts`, tri-state: `null` = unanswered, `true`/`false` = explicit) is answered directly by onboarding Q2 alone. Use `shouldHideOptions()`: options/covered-call UI is hidden **only on an explicit `knows_options === false`** (CC panel, strike alerts, Call % fields, briefing pep talks, Margus options tools + glossary). Unanswered (`null`) **shows** it. This used to read "shown only when `true`", i.e. unanswered hid it too — that stricter rule was disabled outright on 2026-08-18 rather than shipped, and it cannot be restored as written: `shouldSkipExperienceOnboarding` skips the questionnaire as soon as `holdingsCount > 0`, so every existing holder's answer is permanently `null` and treating that as "hide" would strip covered calls from all of them at once, silently. The answer is changeable either way in Account. A "very experienced" investor can validly have zero options experience — never derive one flag from the other.
- **The bottom dock is one well, one cell per destination — do not build it around sheets.** `BookModeDock` draws the app sections, then one cell per portfolio you own, then Circle, all as the same `7.5rem` cell in one floating `.glass` card. **The bar has no band behind it** — the `nav` is a `pointer-events-none` centring container and nothing else; a full-width veil there drew a horizon line across the ambient field and duplicated glass the card already carries. Anything full-width and transparent over content needs `pointer-events-none`, or it swallows every click along the bottom of the page. Sheets carry the day's direction as a dot in the glyph slot so every cell stays the same shape; section labels are the phone's short ones (Home, Pulse, Lab, Growth, Circle); creating a portfolio is the `+` glyph cell opening the same dialog the phone opens — never an inline field in the chrome. **Whether a cell exists may depend on your data, never on which page you are looking at** — the well is centred and content-sized, so one dropped cell resizes and re-centres the whole bar mid-navigation. `src/lib/dock-stability.test.ts` fails if a width-determining prop is wired to a route check; `MobileDock` derives the tier itself (`useHiddenMetaTabIds`) so a new page cannot forget. Folding into one picker cell is decided by `dockFoldsSheets` (`src/lib/dock-cells.ts`) from **both** the cell count and the row's measured width — the seed household has four portfolios, so check any lowered cap against a real book. Full account and measurements in `DESIGN_TOKENS.md` ("The dock").
- **No empty leftover cells in hairline grids.** `gap-px` + `bg-border` paints every CSS grid track. Use `Segmented`, `HairlineGrid`, or `Scoreboard` so the column count always divides the children (`src/lib/filled-grid.ts`). Never hand-roll `grid-cols-N` on that pattern.
- **24-hour clock everywhere — never AM/PM.** Every time a person reads in the app is 24-hour: 08:30 and 20:30, not 8:30 AM / 8:30 PM. Format clock readings through `formatDateTime` (`src/lib/timezone.ts`), which pins `hourCycle: "h23"` so the browser's locale still picks date order and month names but never the hour style. Do not call `toLocaleString`/`toLocaleTimeString` directly for anything with an hour in it, and do not pass `hour12`. `hour: "2-digit"` over `hour: "numeric"` so times line up in `tabular-nums` columns.
- **Listing-currency chips only on mixed books.** `TickerSymbol` shows the USD/EUR/SEK chip only when `listingCurrenciesAreMixed` is true. USD-only and EUR-only books stay chip-free. Share price and average buy stay in the listing's own money; Cost / Value / gain follow the sheet USD/EUR toggle. Table rows never wrap. Mixed books left-align tickers and size that column to the cashtag+chip (`tableCols(n, true)`). Single-currency books keep centered equal columns. Every table row is a fixed `h-10`. Do not add `min-h` or extra vertical padding on cells.
- **Design system: see `DESIGN_TOKENS.md` for the full account.** True-black field (`--background`), shadcn's own card/popover step (`--card`, `oklch(0.205 0 0)` — do not darken it further), hairline borders (`--border`, white at 16% alpha, not a fill). Primary accent is a **subtle warm yellow** (`--primary`), muted and low-chroma on purpose. The only other colours are semantic and fixed: emerald `--gain`, rose `--loss`/`--destructive`, orange `--warning`. Don't reach for a new Tailwind hue decoratively — the Accent Palette in `DESIGN_TOKENS.md` is a ceiling, not a starting point; add to that file first if a fix seems to need a new colour. Two typefaces split by job — Geist for every sentence, Geist Mono for every figure, **Archivo** for headings and the wordmark; heading tracking is a scale, not a constant. Real shadcn primitives (`Card`, `Table`, `Badge`, `Progress`, `Dialog`, `Select`) over hand-rolled divs; semantic tokens (`bg-card`, `border-border`, `text-muted-foreground`) over literal colours; the 4px/8px spacing scale over arbitrary `p-[13px]` values; `font-mono tabular-nums` on every dollar amount, percentage and share count. No tinted card-background washes (`bg-<hue>-950/20` and similar) — status is a border accent or a `Badge`, never a fill. Top-level cards use `.glass` and nested wells `.glass-well` (see `BOX`/`CARD`/`SCORE_CELL` in `Panel.tsx`); don't reintroduce a flat opaque `bg-card`/`bg-muted` on a top-level card, since that is what lets the ambient corner glow read *through* card surfaces rather than only in the gutters. The glow lives on `.page-frame` and follows the tokens automatically; `--ambient-cool` is chrome-only and has no Tailwind utility, so don't reach for it on a component. **Balance the two lobes on Helmholtz–Kohlrausch-corrected lightness (L\*\*), never on brightest channel or luminance** — a saturated blue appears far brighter than a yellow of the same luminance, so a pair that measures balanced can read 19% apart; see `DESIGN_TOKENS.md` for the model and the numbers. **The warm accent cannot be a dark tint** — yellow only reads as yellow while it is light, so a low-alpha `--primary` veil over the near-black field lands on khaki and dim yellow type on it is barely a contrast step. Selected surfaces either fill with `--primary` at full lightness and take `--primary-foreground` type (the dock cell, a `Segmented` item) or stay neutral with `--foreground` type (`WorkspaceSwitcher`, the one selected state that must not compete with the header's CTA). There is no middle. **Nothing that is a surface may be a flat fill** — `bg-muted` as a container background reads as a hole beside a glass card, so use `card-sheen glass-well`. Meter tracks, heatmap and data cells, skeletons, step dots and `focus:bg-muted` stay opaque on purpose. Overlays — menus, selects, popovers, dialogs, sheets, drawers — are `.glass-overlay`, the same material at a much heavier fill (`--popover` at 88% against a card's 45%), because they sit *over* content and an element with `backdrop-filter` does not blur what another one painted, so a menu under the chrome sees the market strip unblurred. Glass carries three specular terms (top hairline, a soft inset falloff under it, bottom hairline); the falloff is the refraction and on a card it is a `box-shadow`, never a `background-image`, because `.veil-hover` would wipe a gradient on hover. `.chrome-pane` is the one exception and uses a gradient on purpose — it is a band of two different heights and a shadow spread is a fixed distance, so it would hug the edge on one and wash half the band on the other; nothing in the chrome hovers.
- **The phone is its own room, not a narrow desktop — full account in `DESIGN_TOKENS.md`.** Below `md` the ambient lobes come in and up, and glass thins, so the corner light reaches through the cards; the phone chrome is **one element**, not one look — `AppHeader`'s `.chrome-pane` holds the phone top bar, the desktop header row and the market strip, because a `backdrop-filter` samples its own slice of the backdrop and two stacked panes land at different tones with a seam between them (`MobileTopBar` is a bare row now; giving it a fill or blur again brings the seam back). The only edge is at the pane's bottom, as on desktop; the page gutter, `PANEL_PAD`, `NESTED_PAD` and `SCORE_CELL` step to 16px below `sm` and figures to `text-xl`. Hard rules: **figures wrap, never `whitespace-nowrap`** (that is how `23.0% a year` escaped its card); **a small card fitting two pairs of values uses two spread rows, never two stacked columns**, sized to fit with `flex-wrap` as the floor (columns share one width, so the `min-w-0` side starves and clips); **a ticker chip heading a column of text gets `.chip-hang`**, opt-in and never on `Badge` itself; put the unit in the sub-line and pass `Scoreboard mobileCols={1}` for any row whose cells carry a sentence. `overscroll-behavior-y: none` on `html, body` is load-bearing — without it WebKit's bounce drags the sticky top bar down and slices the tab bar in half. **When checking for clipping, measure against the nearest clipping ancestor's padding box**, not just `scrollWidth` and the viewport; `.chip-hang` is the one sanctioned exception. Touch targets are pointer-gated in `globals.css` and cover buttons, inputs, selects and `[data-slot="select-trigger"]` — but **never `.inline-edit`** (table rows are a fixed `h-10`) and never the checkbox/switch pseudo (it would steal taps from the rows either side). A size class on an `<h1>`–`<h4>` works now that the element rules are in `@layer base`; `src/lib/heading-scale.test.ts` fails if one disagrees with the scale.
