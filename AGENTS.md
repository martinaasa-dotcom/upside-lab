Do not lower it to "let more glow through" and do not raise it either — past 55% the pane drains the glow instead of attenuating it. **Both apps' docks are one design, and it is two shapes.** Below `md`: full width inside the page gutter, equal cells, glyph over label, labels always on (`MobileTabBar`). At `md` and up: content-hugging, centred, glyph beside label (`BookModeDock`). Upside Arena's `BottomDock` is the same pair. Never hide a dock label at a breakpoint — Arena did, on every phone anybody owns, and five unlabelled glyphs is a puzzle. Cells carry a `PressedFill` (`useLinkStatus`) because every destination waits on the server and an unpressed-looking tab reads as having been ignored; it is drawn behind the cell so it costs no layout, and it never touches `aria-current`. Concentric corners: `rounded-xl` shell, `p-1`, `rounded-lg` cells (12 - 4 = 8). Full account and measurements in `DESIGN_TOKENS.md` ("The dock").
- **A notice pinned to the bottom of the window never types its own height off the bottom.** The cookie question (`AnalyticsConsentBanner`) and the offline chip take `.bottom-notice` and, in the right-hand corner, `.bottom-notice-corner`; the numbers live in `globals.css` and come from what is really drawn down there. The question used to carry `bottom-[max(5.75rem,calc(var(--dock-pad,5.5rem)+5.5rem))]`, which is two guesses stacked: `--dock-pad` is only written once a dock renders, so on the sign-in screen, `/privacy` and `/terms` it fell back to a dock's worth of clearance, added Margus's button on top, and floated the question 176px up an empty page. `use-dock-pad.ts` publishes `data-dock`, `data-bottom-corner` and `--dock-clearance` on `<html>` from a **measurement**, and the CSS reads those. Not a `:has()` selector, and not the route: `WorkspaceShell` keeps every room you have visited mounted behind a `hidden` attribute, and the two docks are `md:hidden` / `hidden md:block`, so "in the DOM" answers yes almost everywhere. Both hooks take the **node**, never a `RefObject` -- the wide dock moves into `WORKSPACE_DOCK_SLOT_ID` through a portal and comes back as a different DOM node, so a ref captured at first layout ends up measuring something detached, which is how the wide screen decided there was no dock and put the cookie card on top of Margus. `src/lib/bottom-notice.test.ts` fails if a notice hardcodes an offset again.
- **No empty leftover cells in hairline grids.** `gap-px` + `bg-border` paints every CSS grid track. Use `Segmented`, `HairlineGrid`, or `Scoreboard` so the column count always divides the children (`src/lib/filled-grid.ts`). Never hand-roll `grid-cols-N` on that pattern.
- **24-hour clock everywhere — never AM/PM.** Every time a person reads in the app is 24-hour: 08:30 and 20:30, not 8:30 AM / 8:30 PM. Format clock readings through `formatDateTime` (`src/lib/timezone.ts`), which pins `hourCycle: "h23"` so the browser's locale still picks date order and month names but never the hour style. Do not call `toLocaleString`/`toLocaleTimeString` directly for anything with an hour in it, and do not pass `hour12`. `hour: "2-digit"` over `hour: "numeric"` so times line up in `tabular-nums` columns.
- **Listing-currency chips only on mixed books.** `TickerSymbol` shows the USD/EUR/SEK chip only when `listingCurrenciesAreMixed` is true. USD-only and EUR-only books stay chip-free. Share price and average buy stay in the listing's own money; Cost / Value / gain follow the portfolio USD/EUR toggle. Table rows never wrap. Mixed books left-align tickers and size that column to the cashtag+chip (`tableCols(n, true)`). Single-currency books keep centered equal columns. Every table row is a fixed `h-10`. Do not add `min-h` or extra vertical padding on cells.
- **The mark is the ten-facet gold "A", and it is drawn in exactly one place.** `src/lib/brand/mark.ts` holds the facet table; `UpsideLogo.tsx`, the favicons, the app icons, the BIMI mark, the email lockup and the OG card all draw from it, and `scripts/generate-pwa-icons.mjs` imports that module rather than rasterising a PNG out of `Images/` (which is now only a record). **The mosaic is the identity — do not replace it with a solid letter.** A branch did exactly that on 2026-08-23 and it was reverted the same day: it needed polish, not redesign. What the polish *is*: the facets are now exactly symmetric about x = 64 (the original was traced and leaned up to 0.75 units, with rows disagreeing with themselves by a tenth of a unit), and the ten near-identical per-facet gradients became four named steps of one ramp — `lit`/`face`/`edge`/`deep`, the same values, grouped. Hairlines shrink with the drawing via `facetScale()`: below 96px the facets swell about their own centroids until the gaps close, because a 3.1-unit cut is three quarters of a pixel at 32px and the mosaic turns to a gold smudge. **Square icons carry no baked corner radius** — iOS draws its own squircle, and every icon here used to arrive pre-rounded, so it got rounded twice; only the shapes nothing masks (favicons, PWA `any`) keep their own corners, and the Apple entry in `layout.tsx` is the 180 square alone. **The mark has two colourways and they are not interchangeable** (`COLOURWAYS`): `MARK` is gold on the app's true black (header, splash, email, OG, `upside-mark.svg`); `ICON` is the reverse — the warm accent as the *field*, facets in deep espresso ink — and it is what every icon, avatar and the BIMI mark use. **An app icon is not chrome.** A near-black tile reads as a hole beside the icons people actually have, so the true-black rule deliberately stops at the icon. Gold also only reads as gold while it is light, so it is either the mark on something very dark or the field — never a mark on a middling warm ground, which is where a 26% warm glow behind a gold mosaic landed (khaki on brown). **Icon plates are one linear gradient, top to bottom** — not the app's radial ambient field, which on a 128px tile is just a smudge. **Coverage is 0.66 of the tile width**, the Apple register (Music ~0.48, Messages ~0.64, Mail ~0.66); 0.80 was tried and read as crowded. Sibling rule: Lab draws **one** peak in gold because a portfolio has nobody else in it; Upside Arena draws **two** in aqua because it is a game against people you know. After changing the geometry run `npm run icons` and bump every `?v=` (layout icons, `OG_IMAGE_PATH`, the email lockup) plus `CACHE` in `public/sw.js`. Full account in `docs/BRAND_MARK.md`.
- **The ambient field is dithered, because eight bits are not enough for it, and the dither's mean must stay exactly zero.** Every lobe in this app is a low-alpha wash on `oklch(0 0 0)`, so the whole ramp is spent in the bottom of the range: measured on the real landing page the warm lobe covers **36 luminance levels of 255 across 840px** and the cool one 31, which is a 1/255 step held flat for **23px**. That is a contour line, not a gradient, and it is the ringing a reader sees around both corners. Re-shaping the stops cannot fix it and neither can a blur (re-quantising a blurred staircase returns the same staircase); the levels are not there to be spent. `AmbientDither` (`src/components/AmbientDither.tsx`, rendered once from the root layout) is the answer, carried by `.page-frame::before`, `.landing-field::after` and `.ambient-glow`. **Signed and clipped, both load-bearing.** Every CSS blend mode that lays grain over a page adds light, and measured lift was +1.6 levels for a `plus-lighter` tile and +2.3 for `overlay`, i.e. the true black the product is built on turned grey; inside a filter chain the noise is added and half of it subtracted again, and both terms are masked by `SourceAlpha` so a pixel the lobes never reach comes back untouched. Lift measures **0.000**, and pure black pixels went **up** from 0.98% of the frame to 1.37%. **Amplitude is two levels and it was measured**: one left the rings findable, three made the grain itself the artefact, two puts a riser's edge at 0.13 of the local pixel spread against 0.29 before. It is **inline, never a data URI** (Safari does not resolve a filter from one, which would leave every iPhone undithered with nothing failing) and one octave at `baseFrequency="0.8"`, so grain rather than clouds. Scroll cost: median frame 16.7ms to 16.8ms. The two amplitude numbers are typed separately and nothing on the page announces it when they drift, so `src/lib/ambient-dither.test.ts` fails if they stop being exact double and half, if either term loses its clip, or if a surface drops the filter. **The glow behind a sample card is `.ambient-glow`, never a hand-rolled ramp** — all three call sites across both apps carried `bg-gradient-to-br from-primary/12 to-transparent opacity-70 blur-3xl`, which through the pane peaked at rgb(27,23,14) and had 22 levels to spend over 760px, so it stepped every 35px in khaki on top of the field's own rings; it is a four-stop falloff now with no blur term. Judge any change here by measuring black lift and riser-to-noise, never by eyeballing. Full account in `DESIGN_TOKENS.md`.
- **Glass is a 2% white veil with all of its light on the rim, and the black inside a pane is the black of the room.** The body is `--glass-veil` (`oklch(1 0 0 / 2%)`), **white and achromatic, never a tinted card**. Two measured reasons. (1) A tinted fill leaves a hue behind: `--card` is a bluish neutral and thinned over a warm field it lands on violet-grey, which is where a mauve cast on a holdings panel came from. Measured on an empty backdrop the old material's own chroma was 1.11 at hue 309; white measures 0. (2) **The veil's alpha is what sets the black floor.** At the old 55%-card fill a pane sat **nine luminance levels out of 255 above the field beside it**; at 2% the lift is −0.1. A pane whose blacks are grey is a card, not glass. **`--glass-contrast` (1.05) is the second lever and is not optional** — contrast pivots on mid-grey so it leaves true black alone, but this field is not true black (the ambient lobes sit at about five levels) and without it a pane lifts those six: measured 1.00 → +6.1, 1.02 → +3.1, 1.05 → +0.1. **Saturation is free**: 1.75 and 2.40 measure identically on black lift, so richness costs nothing. `--glass-blur` is **6px, not 40** — past roughly 7px a blur stops refracting and starts mixing adjacent hues into mud, which is the same mechanism that produced the cast. The rim came down with the fill (`--glass-rim-top` 38%, not 56%) because at this transparency the old weight read as a drawn outline rather than as light; it still takes the room's two hues, warm on top from `--primary`, cool underneath from `--ambient-cool`, with the sides unequal because the key light is off the left. **Wells are panes in their own right** (`--glass-veil-well` 3%, own 5px blur, the softest rim in the system) rather than grey boxes on a pane, because forty holdings rows must not add up to a texture. **Overlays and the dock are the surfaces that keep a heavy fill and a hard blur** — they sit over real content, which is both why they must soften it and where a heavy blur earns its keep (`.glass-overlay` at `--popover` 78%, `.glass-dock` at `--background` 55%; the dock's is field-keyed, so its alpha is exactly how much of the ambient glow it eats and it cannot leave a hue behind). Never reintroduce a falloff, a `background-image` ramp, or a tinted card fill on any glass surface. Judge a change by measuring black lift and the material's own chroma on real surfaces, never by eyeballing.
- **Design system: see `DESIGN_TOKENS.md` for the full account.** True-black field (`--background`), shadcn's own card/popover step (`--card`, `oklch(0.205 0 0)` — do not darken it further), hairline borders (`--border`, white at 16% alpha, not a fill). Primary accent is a **subtle warm yellow** (`--primary`), muted and low-chroma on purpose. The only other colours are semantic and fixed: emerald `--gain`, rose `--loss`/`--destructive`, orange `--warning`. Don't reach for a new Tailwind hue decoratively — the Accent Palette in `DESIGN_TOKENS.md` is a ceiling, not a starting point; add to that file first if a fix seems to need a new colour. Two typefaces split by job — Geist for every sentence, Geist Mono for every figure, **Archivo** for headings and the wordmark; heading tracking is a scale, not a constant. Real shadcn primitives (`Card`, `Table`, `Badge`, `Progress`, `Dialog`, `Select`) over hand-rolled divs; semantic tokens (`bg-card`, `border-border`, `text-muted-foreground`) over literal colours; the 4px/8px spacing scale over arbitrary `p-[13px]` values; **a `Panel` spaces its own children (`gap-5 sm:gap-6`), so a direct child must not add `mt-*`/`mb-*` — it gets both** (a title and its subtitle are one wrapped child, and a component never carries its own outer margin); `font-mono tabular-nums` on every dollar amount, percentage and share count. No tinted card-background washes (`bg-<hue>-950/20` and similar) — status is a border accent or a `Badge`, never a fill. Top-level cards use `.glass` and nested wells `.glass-well` (see `BOX`/`CARD`/`SCORE_CELL` in `Panel.tsx`); don't reintroduce a flat opaque `bg-card`/`bg-muted` on a top-level card, since that is what lets the ambient corner glow read *through* card surfaces rather than only in the gutters. The glow lives on `.page-frame` and follows the tokens automatically; `--ambient-cool` is chrome-only and has no Tailwind utility, so don't reach for it on a component. **Balance the two lobes on Helmholtz–Kohlrausch-corrected lightness (L\*\*), never on brightest channel or luminance** — a saturated blue appears far brighter than a yellow of the same luminance, so a pair that measures balanced can read 19% apart; see `DESIGN_TOKENS.md` for the model and the numbers. **The warm accent cannot be a dark tint** — yellow only reads as yellow while it is light, so a low-alpha `--primary` veil over the near-black field lands on khaki and dim yellow type on it is barely a contrast step. Selected surfaces either fill with `--primary` at full lightness and take `--primary-foreground` type (the dock cell, a `Segmented` item) or stay neutral with `--foreground` type (`WorkspaceSwitcher`, the one selected state that must not compete with the header's CTA). There is no middle. **Nothing that is a surface may be a flat fill** — `bg-muted` as a container background reads as a hole beside a glass card, so use `card-sheen glass-well`. Meter tracks, heatmap and data cells, skeletons, step dots and `focus:bg-muted` stay opaque on purpose. Overlays — menus, selects, popovers, dialogs, sheets, drawers — are `.glass-overlay`, the same material at a much heavier fill (`--popover` at 88% against a card's 45%), because they sit *over* content and an element with `backdrop-filter` does not blur what another one painted, so a menu under the chrome sees the market strip unblurred. Glass carries three specular terms (top hairline, a soft inset falloff under it, bottom hairline); the falloff is the refraction and on a card it is a `box-shadow`, never a `background-image`, because `.veil-hover` would wipe a gradient on hover. `.chrome-pane` is the one exception and uses a gradient on purpose — it is a band of two different heights and a shadow spread is a fixed distance, so it would hug the edge on one and wash half the band on the other; nothing in the chrome hovers.
- **The phone is its own room, not a narrow desktop — full account in `DESIGN_TOKENS.md`.** Below `md` the ambient lobes come in and up, and glass thins, so the corner light reaches through the cards; the phone chrome is **one element**, not one look — `AppHeader`'s `.chrome-pane` holds the phone top bar, the desktop header row and the market strip, because a `backdrop-filter` samples its own slice of the backdrop and two stacked panes land at different tones with a seam between them (`MobileTopBar` is a bare row now; giving it a fill or blur again brings the seam back). The only edge is at the pane's bottom, as on desktop; the page gutter, `PANEL_PAD`, `NESTED_PAD` and `SCORE_CELL` step to 16px below `sm` and figures to `text-xl`. Hard rules: **figures wrap, never `whitespace-nowrap`** (that is how `23.0% a year` escaped its card); **a small card fitting two pairs of values uses two spread rows, never two stacked columns**, sized to fit with `flex-wrap` as the floor (columns share one width, so the `min-w-0` side starves and clips); **a ticker chip heading a column of text gets `.chip-hang`**, opt-in and never on `Badge` itself; put the unit in the sub-line and pass `Scoreboard mobileCols={1}` for any row whose cells carry a sentence. `overscroll-behavior-y: none` on `html, body` is load-bearing — without it WebKit's bounce drags the sticky top bar down and slices the tab bar in half. **When checking for clipping, measure against the nearest clipping ancestor's padding box**, not just `scrollWidth` and the viewport; `.chip-hang` is the one sanctioned exception. Touch targets are pointer-gated in `globals.css` and cover buttons, inputs, selects and `[data-slot="select-trigger"]` — but **never `.inline-edit`** (table rows are a fixed `h-10`) and never the checkbox/switch pseudo (it would steal taps from the rows either side). A size class on an `<h1>`–`<h4>` works now that the element rules are in `@layer base`; `src/lib/heading-scale.test.ts` fails if one disagrees with the scale.
